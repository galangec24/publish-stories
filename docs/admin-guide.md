# Admin guide

This guide covers everyday tasks on **Publish Stories**. Sign in at https://publish-stories-ph.web.app, then open your picture (top right) → **Studio & admin**.

> **Tip:** if the site takes about a minute to open after a quiet spell, the free server is waking up. It's normal.

## Stories

### Write a new story
1. **Stories → New story**. Choose **Write** (start blank) or **Upload a manuscript** (Word .docx, .md or .txt).
2. Fill in the title, genres, column (optional) and price:
   - Leave the price at ₱0 for a free story. Paid stories cost at least ₱20.
   - Choose how many chapters are **free** before readers pay.
3. Click **Create**. The **Story Studio** opens.

### In the Story Studio
- **Chapters** are listed on the left (Navigation):
  - **+** adds a chapter.
  - Hover a chapter to **rename** or **delete** it. Deleted chapters go to **Recently deleted** (the box-with-arrow button at the top of the list) for 30 days; **Restore** puts one back where it was.
  - Drag a chapter to reorder it.
  - Each chapter needs a different name.
- **The lock next to each chapter** decides what readers get free:
  - Green 🔓 means free, amber 🔒 means paid.
  - Clicking a lock frees that chapter and every chapter before it, or locks it and every chapter after it.
  - Free chapters always start from Chapter 1.
- **Unlock all chapters** (top of the chapter list) makes the whole story free for a while, for example as a promotion. Turn it off and the chapter locks come back exactly as you left them.
- **The ribbon** has bold, headings, lists, alignment, font and size, pictures, find, word count and a page split. Your work saves automatically every few seconds.
- **Story settings** cover title, synopsis, cover picture, genres, column, price, free chapters and reading fonts.
- **Publish** asks for the price one last time. You can change the price until the first reader pays; after that it is locked.

### Upload many manuscripts at once
Go to **Bulk import**, drop the files in, set the shared genre, price and free chapters, and choose whether to publish straight away.

A test manuscript is in `samples/ang-parola-sa-samal.docx`: four chapters with a picture.

## Getting paid

### Your QR codes (one-time setup)
1. **Your QR codes → Add QR code**, then upload the QR image from GCash (Receive → QR) or the bank app.
2. The form fills itself in: name, account holder and masked number. Check it and **Save**.
3. Each card should say **"Amount fills in when readers scan"** in green.

### Checking payments (every day)
When a reader pays, the story opens for them **right away**. You check afterwards.
1. Open **QR payments**. New payments are under **To check**, with a red count in the menu.
2. Tap the **receipt** to see it full size.
3. In your GCash or BPI app, find the incoming transfer with the **same reference number**, and check that the amount matches the story's price.
4. Press **Confirm received**. If you can't find it, or it's short, press **Revoke**. That locks the story again for that reader, and they are told why.

Use the **search box** to find a payment by reference number, name, email, story or access code.

### Sales report
**Sales report** shows money received by day, week, month or year:
- pick stories, or leave it on **All stories**;
- set the dates;
- read the total, the average per sale, the top story, and the amounts not yet checked or revoked.

**Export CSV** opens the figures in Excel.

## Readers

- **Readers & payments:** who read a paid story without paying, and open checkouts.
- **Comments:** hide or delete comments. Your own comments show an **Admin** badge.
- **Support inbox:** reply to readers. You can send emoji, GIFs, pictures and files.
  - Readers without an account can write too. They show as **Guest**, with the name and email they left (if any).
  - If a guest left an email, they also get your reply by email. Otherwise they see it the next time they open the chat on the same phone or computer.
- **Email requests:** approve or deny readers who want to change their email.

## The website itself

- **About me:** edit your photo, introduction, roles, links and the "From the Author's Desk" text.
- **Genres:** add, rename or remove. Removing a genre doesn't delete its stories.
- **Columns:** each column is a link to your column on SunStar. Give it a title, a short description and the SunStar link. It appears as a card in **Her columns** on the home page and in the **Columns** tab, separate from your stories, and opens SunStar in a new tab.
- **Activity log:** every change made in the admin area, with who, what and when.

## Readers' side, in short

- The first chapters are free. To unlock the rest, a reader:
  1. scans your QR (the price is already in it);
  2. pays;
  3. taps **I've paid**, attaches the receipt screenshot and types the reference number.
- No account is needed. The story opens on their device, on other devices on the same Wi-Fi, and anywhere they enter their **access code**.
- Readers can like, comment and rate. Readers without an account can rate after reading every chapter.
- **My purchases** (menu or footer) shows whether each payment went through.
